What we check

  • Your database: Supabase tables without row-level security, rules that let anyone change every row, functions that run any SQL, and Firebase rules open to anyone.
  • Your keys: the Supabase admin key, OpenAI or Gemini keys in the website, secrets in VITE_ / NEXT_PUBLIC_ settings, passwords written into the code, and keys left anywhere in your project's history.
  • Your code: SQL and NoSQL injection, visitor input shown unescaped (XSS), commands built from what visitors type, files opened from a name they send, unlocked login cookies, and more, in JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Java, Go, Ruby, PHP and C#.
  • Who can reach what: records anyone can open by changing an ID, admin rights taken from what the browser sends, forms that save whatever is sent, debug pages that leak users, personal data readable by anyone, and server functions that let strangers spend your AI credits.
  • The basics: known vulnerabilities in your libraries, whether your tests pass, and whether your live site is up and secure.

How it works

  1. Send a link to your repository (read-only access is enough) or a zip of the code.
  2. Within 48 hours you get a report like this sample: every problem with the file, the line and the code, why it matters and what to do. Keys are never shown in full.
  3. Want it fixed? Each fix arrives as a reviewed change you approve, with a record of what checked it. Nothing touches your app without your yes.

Your code stays yours

  • The check runs on our own machine with fixed rules. No AI service reads your code to produce the report, and the same code always gets the same result.
  • Your code is deleted when the work is done, and never shared or used for anything else.
  • What it does not do: it is not a full penetration test, and it cannot judge your business logic. The report says plainly what was and was not checked.
